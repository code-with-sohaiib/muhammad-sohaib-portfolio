About Me

I build the systems that get businesses found

I'm Muhammad Sohaib, a Local SEO specialist and WordPress developer. My work sits at the intersection of visibility and conversion: getting a business found on Google Search and Google Maps, then giving it a website built to turn that attention into customers.

What drives me is simple — a website or a search ranking only matters if it moves a business forward. Every project starts with a business goal, not a design trend, and every recommendation I make is one I can tie back to rankings, calls, or leads.

I keep pace with how Google's algorithms and WordPress's ecosystem evolve, because standing still in this field means falling behind. Whether I'm untangling a technical SEO issue or debugging a slow page, I approach it the same way: identify the real problem, fix it properly, and measure the result.
